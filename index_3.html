Getting there

~1–1¼ hrs from Cape Town

On the R45 between Boschendal and Franschhoek, on the Berg River (part of Two Rivers). Two entrances: use the West Gate in summer; the East Gate skips the low river bridge if it's wet.

The West Gate is directly opposite the Franschhoek Motor Museum (pinned above). Tap a gate below to open the Farmstead's exact pin in Google Maps, then hit “Directions”.

📍 West Gate (summer) — open in Maps 📍 East Gate (wet-weather)

Summer route · West Gate

  1. From Cape Town: the N1, then the R45 toward Franschhoek.
  2. 14 km before Franschhoek, opposite the Franschhoek Motor Museum, is the Two Rivers West Gate.
  3. Through the gate, stay on the tar ~1 km (don't take the gravel road right to Elkasun, or the brick road left).
  4. Over the hump and down to the low-level bridge — there's always a few inches of water over it; it's fine, just cross slowly.
  5. The Farmstead is 50 m on the left.

Wet-weather route · East Gate

  1. Rather skip the river bridge? Use the East Gate (same gate code).
  2. From the West Gate, continue ~5 km on the R45 toward Franschhoek, past Bridge House School, across the big bridge.
  3. Turn immediately left (about 10 m after the bridge) to the Two Rivers East Gate.
  4. The Central Gate beyond it has a different code — call ahead if you'll use that one.
